Public Mac electrical route check: 1.6.27 build 99

Tested by the PolyPDF publisher, Euclidean Software LLC, on November 5, 2026 (US Eastern time). On Willow Creek E101, the drawn LP-1 / CKTS 1, 3, 5, 7 path measured 46 ft (8 ft north + 38 ft east), matching the 46.00 ft centerline printed on the sheet. This is drawn circuit-diagram geometry, not a conduit or conductor quantity. The fixture and receptacle counts were not run: they need Pro Symbol Search or more than Free’s allowance, and this test used Free.

Repeat the check

  1. Open Willow Creek E101 · source PDF and set the scale from its 20 ft reference with Snap to Content on.
  2. With Path Length, start at the top of panel LP-1, turn at the corridor, and finish at the east arrow. Press Enter to finish.
  3. Export the worksheet CSV, save the PDF, quit and reopen the app, open the saved PDF, and compare the total.

Read this number correctly

  • The sheet labels this dashed line a circuit diagram. Actual routing, rises, drops, terminations, fittings, slack and conductor counts come from your project documents.
  • 46 ft is not an order quantity for conduit or wire, and this check says nothing about circuit design or code compliance.
  • The CSV’s lower Group/Value block repeats the row above it. Do not add them together.

What this record does not cover

  • This record covers build 99 only. Later PolyPDF releases were not part of it.
  • This is a publisher test, not independent certification. It used the public Mac 1.6.27 build 99 download on a MacBook Air with Apple silicon and macOS 26.4.1, with a fresh user profile and no license key entered, so Free limits applied. On this test Mac the Upgrade window showed a notice that the app could not confirm a Pro license.
  • The test used automated control of the app’s normal interface. The files offered here are as the app saved them. Dates stored inside the PDF are in UTC, so they read November 6.
  • The saved PDF was reopened in PolyPDF after quitting and reopening the app, and was also opened in Apple Preview. Adobe Acrobat and other PDF viewers were not checked.
  • Symbol Search and other Pro features were not tested. Keep this separate from any statement about automatic counting.
  • The drawing is synthetic and not for construction.
