Repeat the check

  1. Record the installed version, build and operating system. This test used the unmodified public Mac 1.6.27 build 99 DMG, with a separate ordinary profile and no test license or bypass.
  2. Open Willow Creek A101, Revision A · source PDF. The two-page drawing is synthetic and not for construction.
  3. Calibrate the printed 20 ft graphic reference. The test span was 360 PDF points, giving 18 PDF points per foot. Check the independent 96 ft and 60 ft dimensions before measuring area.
  4. Trace the outside footprint. Export the worksheet as CSV and the takeoff summary as PDF. Save a working PDF copy, close it, reopen it, and verify calibration and quantities.
  5. Quit and relaunch the app, reopen the saved copy, and repeat the checks. Switch Metric then Imperial: the test displayed 29,260.80 mm, 18,288.00 mm and 535.12 sq m before returning to the original Imperial values.

Read the CSV correctly

The first table contains one area row and one grouped length row. The lower Group/Value summary repeats those quantities by subject; adding both tables would double count them. The 156 ft total is the sum of two independent dimensions, not the 312 ft perimeter. The 5,760 sq ft footprint includes walls and is not net installed flooring or a complete estimate.

Known limitations

  • The area caption overlaps the underlying corridor label in the saved drawing; this PDF is retained for QA, not distributed here. Numeric results and the CSV are unaffected.
  • The fourth hand-created measurement was refused with the Free three-measurement limit. A local Pro label also appeared in Upgrade; entitlement provenance was not established. No test license, license key entry or activation was used. This does not verify Pro activation or Pro workflows.
  • Windows, other PDF viewers, net flooring quantities, symbol counts, electrical/flooring trade workflows, and complete estimates were not verified in this test.
  • The worksheet summary repeats the same quantities by subject; do not add it to the detailed rows. The 156 ft sum is 96 + 60 ft, not a perimeter. Gross area includes walls.
