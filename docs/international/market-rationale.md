# Language and search rationale — September 8, 2026

The initial language set balances substantial construction activity with coverage of several
countries per maintained language. This is an editorial prioritization, not a ranking of
construction spending or a forecast of PolyPDF customers. We have not claimed country-level
conversion demand without Search Console or sales evidence.

- Spanish supports Mexico and other Spanish-speaking construction teams. The US International
  Trade Administration's [Mexico construction guide](https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/mexico-construction)
  describes its construction and infrastructure market.
- Arabic supports Saudi Arabia and Arabic-speaking teams in the Gulf and Egypt. ITA's
  [Saudi design and construction briefing](https://www.trade.gov/market-intelligence/saudi-arabia-design-and-construction)
  identifies demand for construction technology around housing, infrastructure and major projects.
- Portuguese addresses Brazil. Brazil's Ministry of Transport publishes its
  [transport infrastructure investment programme](https://www.gov.br/transportes/pt-br/assuntos/concessoes/RoadShow4aEd_2025ENapresentacao16.05.2025.pdf).
- German and French cover continental European and Canadian audiences. ITA's
  [AEC services report](https://www.trade.gov/report/architecture-engineering-and-construction-aec-services)
  discusses international architectural, engineering and construction services, including Germany,
  France and Canada. Language reach is our inference, not a measured software market share.
- Hindi adds an option for Indian readers; English remains available. The government-backed
  [India Investment Grid construction overview](https://indiainvestmentgrid.gov.in/sectors/construction)
  describes infrastructure-driven activity. Hindi is not a substitute for all Indian languages.
- Simplified Chinese supports Chinese readers; ITA's AEC services report includes China among
  major cross-border construction service markets. No claim is made about search-provider
  accessibility, procurement eligibility or payments in any specific country.
- Japanese supports Japan. The Statistics Bureau's
  [construction yearbook](https://www.stat.go.jp/english/data/nenkan/74nenkan/1431-10.html)
  provides official construction investment and completed-work statistics.

Implementation follows Google's documentation for
[localized versions](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/localized-versions),
[multilingual sites](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/managing-multi-regional-sites),
and [AI features](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features): discoverable
URLs, useful visible text, accurate structured data, and language alternatives. There is no
special AEO schema that guarantees AI citations. Existing quick answers, practical steps,
FAQs, source links and limitations are translated in full instead of creating thin keyword pages.
